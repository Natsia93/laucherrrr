# ✍️ Natural Human Writing & Anti-AI Slop Rules (Launcher OS)

Aturan ini dibuat berdasarkan preferensi gaya penulisan manusia yang bersih, natural, langsung pada sasaran (*plain & direct*), serta daftar tanda tulisan AI (*AI writing tells / AI slop*) yang wajib **DIHAPUS dan DIHINDARI 100%** di seluruh hasil generasi Launcher OS.

---

## 1. Hard Rules (Prinsip Baku Penulisan)

1. **NO EM DASHES (—):**
   - Dilarang keras menggunakan em dash (`—`) untuk jeda kalimat.
   - Gunakan tanda koma (`,`), tanda kurung `(...)`, atau pecah menjadi kalimat baru.

2. **NO "NOT X BUT Y" PATTERNS:**
   - Dilarang memakai pola klise:
     - *"not X but Y"*
     - *"it's not X, it's Y"*
     - *"X isn't about A, it's about B"*
   - Hindari juga bentuk samaran polanya:
     - *"Usage tells you A. It says nothing about B."*
     - *"You don't need X. You need Y."*
     - *"The reason is rarely X..."*
   - Langsung nyatakan faktanya secara lugas dan positif.

3. **NO COLON-REVEAL RHYTHM IN PROSE:**
   - Dilarang membuat kalimat bergaya panggung/teatrikal dengan titik dua di tengah paragraf:
     - ❌ *"Three things matter: speed, cost and quality."*
     - ❌ *"The answer: operational discipline."*
   - Catatan: Titik dua hanya boleh digunakan saat mengawali daftar bullet points biasa.

4. **CONTRACTIONS & DIRECT WORDS:**
   - Gunakan kata-kata yang wajar dan bersahaja (*contractions are fine*). Hindari kata-kata yang dipersulit demi terdengar akademis/sok pintar.

5. **TITLE CASING:**
   - Gunakan **Sentence case** untuk judul slide presentasi (huruf kapital hanya di awal dan nama diri).
   - **Title Case** hanya untuk judul artikel/buku.

## 2. 17 Tanda Tulisan AI yang Wajib Dihapus (AI Tells Checklist)

Setiap draft wajib bersih dari 17 karakteristik AI ini:

1. **One-line dramatic paragraphs & verbless fragments:**
   - ❌ *"The customer still waits for Thursday."*
   - ❌ *"Same tools, different outcomes."*
2. **Antithesis or aphorism closers:**
   - ❌ *"A faster memo supports a small claim. A faster decision supports a budget."*
   - ❌ *"A demo gets a demo's budget."*
3. **Rule of three everywhere:**
   - Jangan seragamkan setiap poin menjadi 3 item berirama sama: ❌ *"The model worked, the analysts used it, the drafts were good."*
   - Gunakan pasangan (2 poin), 4 poin, atau daftar natural sesuai isi sebenarnya.
4. **Rhetorical question answered immediately:**
   - ❌ *"So how would the bank do it differently? Roughly like this."*
5. **Fake-scenario openers:**
   - ❌ *"Picture this..."*, *"Imagine a world where..."*, *"Let me make it concrete"*.
6. **Filler intensifiers:**
   - Hindari kata penguat murahan: *actually, genuinely, simply, truly, really, quite, incredibly, very often*.
7. **Repeated parallel "Bold lead-in. Explanation." blocks:**
   - Jangan seragamkan seluruh kartu/bullet dengan panjang kalimat yang kaku dan simetris persis.
8. **Section closers that restate the section:**
   - ❌ *"In conclusion"*, *"Ultimately"*, *"At the end of the day"*, *"None of these is a technology problem..."*.
9. **Clever mirrored or metaphor phrasing:**
   - ❌ *"holds the work up somewhere the pilot isn't looking"*, *"built the plumbing once"*.
10. **Stock AI idioms:**
    - Hindari: *sits (outside/at), lands, surfaces, worth noting/copying, in public, beside the point, I'd be lying if, here's the thing, the real X, quietly, load-bearing, starves, unlock (insight), double down*.
11. **Banned AI Buzzwords:**
    - ❌ *delve, leverage, foster, robust, seamless, landscape, ecosystem, realm, tapestry, journey, navigate, pivotal, crucial, multifaceted, comprehensive, holistic, testament, transformative, game-changer, cutting-edge*.
12. **Cliche transitions:**
    - ❌ *furthermore, moreover, additionally, it's important to note, it's worth noting*.
13. **Set-up then reveal rhythm:**
    - ❌ *"...came back to the same thing. What's the return?"*
14. **Too many bracketed asides:**
    - Batasi penggunaan tanda kurung tambahan agar alur baca tetap bersih.
15. **Vague attribution:**
    - ❌ *"studies show"*, *"experts say"*. Wajib sebutkan sumbernya atau tulis fakta apa adanya.
16. **Hedge stacks:**
    - ❌ *"could potentially"*, *"may eventually"*. Nyatakan probabilitas secara definitif.
17. **Uniform sentence length & robotic perfection:**
    - Variasikan ritme kalimat: gabungkan kalimat pendek dan kalimat sedang layaknya manusia menulis.
